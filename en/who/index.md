<p>Hi! I'm <strong>Jyri-Petteri Paloposki</strong>, a 36 year old IT entrepreneur and non-profit fiend. I moved to Turku from Hämeenlinna in autumn 2009 to begin with my computer science studies in the University of Turku. The Turku digital media scene got a hold of me pretty soon and I got a bit side tracked from my studies – after a short stint as a freelancer I worked full-time on the field for almost ten years. I graduated as a bachelor of science last year and am continuing my studies now at the University of Turku for a MSc in Computer Science and the University of Vaasa for a MSc in Economics. Among my studies I'm also working as an entrepreneur working on web service development for large public organisations and a bunch of other IT things.</p><p>I've been an active scout for somewhat my whole life, and shortly after moving to Turku I ended up in Turun Sinikotkat, a local scout group, in which I've volunteered a number of years as a cub scout leader and also a few years as a local group leader, among other things. Forward a few years to 2018 to 2020, during which I've volunteered in the board of the Scouts and Guides of Finland, the largest youth organisation in Finland, where I was responsible for our finance sector, which also contains our information systems and private fundraising.</p><p>I am and have been also active in a number of student organisations, including the computer science student's
